# Google Form Setup: Intent-to-Silicon Human Evaluation

To prove to the reviewers that our Intent-to-Silicon engine maps to what *real humans* want, we need 50 users to fill out a simple survey. 

## Form Structure

**Title**: AI Coding Agent User Validation Study
**Description**: Please tell us what you think the user actually means when they say the following phrases. Pick the best category for each.

---
