# AAYU Brand Identity & Guidelines

## The Official Logo
The AAYU official logo is a high-definition, stylized capital letter **'A'**.
- **Left Hemisphere:** A white/grey human brain, representing Human Intent, AI, and autonomous thought.
- **Right Hemisphere:** A glowing red silicon circuit board with electrical traces, representing Hardware, execution, and silicon.
- **Background:** Pitch black.

**THIS LOGO IS PERMANENTLY LOCKED AS THE OFFICIAL IDENTITY OF THE AAYU PROGRAMMING LANGUAGE.**

### Usage Restrictions
1. Do not alter the colors of the brain or the silicon circuit.
2. Do not change the pitch-black background.
3. This logo must be used on all official AAYU platforms, including the VS Code Extension, PyPI, GitHub, and the Official Website.

## Brand Philosophy
AAYU stands for **Intent to Silicon**. The logo perfectly captures this transition—from human thought (brain) directly to machine execution (circuit)—without the boilerplate in between.
